# Legal source collection and maintenance

## Official starting points
Follow each portal to the exact Act, Rules, Gazette notification, amendment, circular, prescribed form or judgment. Confirm the destination is official and current before ingestion.

- India Code — Central Acts and legislation: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/
- e-Gazette of India — Gazette notifications: https://egazette.gov.in/
- Legislative Department, Ministry of Law and Justice: https://legislative.gov.in/
- Ministry of Labour & Employment: https://labour.gov.in/
- Department of Consumer Affairs: https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/
- Supreme Court of India: https://www.sci.gov.in/
- National Legal Services Authority: https://nalsa.gov.in/
- Karnataka State Legal Services Authority: https://kslsa.kar.nic.in/

These are starting points, not a claim that every document is present in this project. Relevant High Courts, State departments, commissions, tribunals and local authorities may publish additional material.

## Ingestion checklist
1. Record official URL, title, publisher, jurisdiction, publication/effective date, language and licence/access terms.
2. Identify whether it is an amendment, consolidated/repealed version, subordinate rule, notification, circular, judgment or prescribed form.
3. Check commencement, territorial scope, amendments, repeal/supersession and later corrigenda.
4. Upload through Knowledge Base. It is stored as `pending_review` and is not used by RAG by default.
5. A designated reviewer must inspect the source and approve it. Approval is a workflow flag, not a legal correctness guarantee.
6. Periodically check publishers for amendments, new notifications and replacement forms. Maintain version history and withdraw superseded materials.
7. Test questions against authoritative sources and verify each returned citation/excerpt manually.

Third-party legal datasets may be incomplete, stale, duplicated, OCR-corrupted or restricted by licence. Preserve provenance/licence information. Never treat generated summaries or synthetic fixtures as law.
